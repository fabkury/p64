The intro animations release.

- 21 boot animations of the logo, a random one at each power-on, or a chosen one (Settings > Display).
- Play now and Play next on the Settings page preview them on the panel.
- API: `GET /api/v1/boot_animations`, `POST /api/v1/action/boot_animation`, `GET /api/v1/diag/intro_bench`.

Update from v0.2.0 on the device's Update page. Flash from the browser at https://fabkury.github.io/p64/web-flasher/ or with esptool: https://github.com/fabkury/p64/blob/main/docs/flash-firmware.md
