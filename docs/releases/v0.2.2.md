A robustness release: five defects found by the first abuse tests.

- Rapid previous/next from several browser tabs could stop the show until a playset was chosen again; navigation bursts are now coalesced.
- A malformed request (deeply nested JSON) rebooted the device; it is answered 400.
- After a crash the status document could be invalid text; the crash summary is sanitised.
- A like sent while the Makapix worker was busy for over 20 s could corrupt memory.
- The setup portal's save and erase routes answered on a running device, outside the PIN; they answer only in setup mode.

Update from v0.2.1 on the device's Update page. Flash from the browser at https://fabkury.github.io/p64/web-flasher/ or with esptool: https://github.com/fabkury/p64/blob/main/docs/flash-firmware.md
