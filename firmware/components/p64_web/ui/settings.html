1 to 255. Full white at 255 needs the 27 W supply on the POWER port.

Inside the window the panel uses the night brightness (or turns off); a window may cross midnight.

    About p64
    
        
    AuthorFabrício Kury
    
        
    Sourcegithub.com/fabkury/p64
    
        
    HardwareWaveshare ESP32-S3-RGB-Matrix + RGB-Matrix-P2-64x64
    
        
    Built on ESP-IDF (Apache-2.0), esphome/esp-hub75 (with the p64 patch), bitbank2/AnimatedGIF (Apache-2.0), libpng, libwebp (BSD), zlib, cJSON (MIT), Open-Meteo weather data; the web UI shares its stylesheet and themes with p3a. Pixel fonts: Capital Hill and Everyday Typical (bundled with their authors' licences in assets/fonts).
