# Extra Thick
Extra Thick is a bold 17pt pixel art font designed with very wide and thick looking lettering. 

## Author
[VEXED](https://v3x3d.itch.io/)

## Basic Info
- Base Glyph Height: 17px
- Scales: 17, 34, 51, 68, etc
- Ascender Max: 12px
- Descender Max: 5px

## Kerning
None
