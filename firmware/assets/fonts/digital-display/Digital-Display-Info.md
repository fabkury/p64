# Digital Display
Digital Display is a 19x15 pixel font designed to mimic digital clock fonts while having a bit more flexability in design.

## Author
[VEXED](https://v3x3d.itch.io/)

## Basic Info
- Base Glyph Height: 19px
- Scales: 19, 38, 57, 76, etc.
- Ascender Max: 8px
- Descender Max: 6px
