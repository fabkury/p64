1. p64a and p64b are not "prototypes", but finished as designed and working very well. The firmware is in version 0.2.0 and in good working condition (I have 2 p64 devices running basically 24/7) but should remain under development for an unspecified amount of time.

2. Page https://github.com/fabkury/p64/blob/main/docs/status.md contains innacuracies. Another example, "Makapix views refused with HTTP 429 are retried at the next swap" is not an "outstanding check" but an "engineering note" of sorts -- the same goes for "While pairing, the device polls the server for its credentials often enough to draw 'HTTP 429 Too many credential requests'".
