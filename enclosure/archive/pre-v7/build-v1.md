# Legacy v1 build notes

For an existing v1 shell. New builds should follow the [v7 guide](../../../docs/build-your-own.md).

The v1 shell has no knobs or USB-C adapter cradle. Its
[print file](output/v1/p64_enclosure_print.stl) uses a right-angle (up/down angled)
USB-C cable inserted from underneath. After installing the controller, power lead and
panel, fit six M3×10 screws and lay the cable in the groove toward the rear notch.
No v7 frame-notch or glued-insert step is needed.

The shared electronics, firmware flashing and first-run instructions are in the
[build guide](../../../docs/build-your-own.md). Original geometry and print history
remain in the [archive README](README.md). The first v1 print was black FDM PLA,
ordered from JLC3DP for $18.83 delivered; it used the larger-clearance service file
and had slight panel side play.
